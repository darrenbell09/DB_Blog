Approach

Three principles.

IT infrastructure is often treated as overhead. Designed and operated well, technology is a strategic capability that reduces risk, controls cost, and lets the organization move with confidence.

  • Operational discipline over technical elegance

    The system that runs reliably at 2am matters more than the one that looked great in the design doc.

  • Business outcomes over technical metrics

    The CFO cares about the bill and the uptime, not the architecture diagram.

  • Cost as a first-class concern

    Waste is an operational discipline problem that compounds over time.

Areas of expertise

IT Operations & Service Delivery

Running IT operations means owning the outcome, not just the technology. Incident response, change control, vendor management, and SLA accountability, in environments where downtime has real consequences.

Technology Leadership & Team Development

Leading IT functions across healthcare and managed services: owning budgets, building teams, managing vendors, and communicating to executives, compliance, and operations.

Microsoft 365 Architecture

Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint, Intune, and Defender for M365, administered and architected so the environment is defensible, auditable, and operationally maintainable.

Azure Cloud Design

Entra ID, storage architecture, Azure Virtual Desktop, analytics infrastructure, and cloud cost governance, where compliance is non-negotiable and cost efficiency is ongoing work.

AI in Operations

Hands-on with AI in the day-to-day: Copilot, automation, and putting models to work where they change how the team operates, not as a side experiment.

Cloud Cost Optimization

Licensing audits, environment rationalization, and storage tier optimization. Cost problems are almost always operational discipline problems: visibility, ownership, and accountability.

Why this site

There is no shortage of vendor content and conference-polished success stories. What is harder to find is honest writing about what it actually takes to run compliant, cost-efficient technology operations: the budget battles, the compliance pressures, the decisions that look obvious in hindsight.

If you're a cloud architect, IT manager, or senior engineer navigating similar terrain, I hope you find something useful here.
